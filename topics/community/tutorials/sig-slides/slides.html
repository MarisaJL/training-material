- Special Interest Groups are represented on the Galaxy Community Board - Working Groups are represented on the Galaxy Technical Board - The GCB and GTB send representatives to the Galaxy Executive Board --- ## What is a Special Interest Group (SIG)? - SIGs are groups of Galaxy users and contributors who share interests - Most SIGs focus on a scientific field or technique - Some SIGs are regional communities - SIGs enable community members to collaborate and help each other - Find out more and see a list of current SIGs (here)[https://galaxyproject.org/community/sig/] --- ## What is the Galaxy Community Board (GCB)? - The GCB represents users in the Galaxy governance - Each SIG can send a representative to the GCB - The GTB chair is a member of the Galaxy Executive Board --- ## What is a Working Group (WG)? - Working Groups are communities that focus on specific technical areas - Systems - Backend - User Interface / User Experience - Testing & Hardening - Workflows - GOATs (Galaxy Outreach And Training & Support) - Committers - Find out more and see a list of current SIGs (here)[https://galaxyproject.org/community/wg/] --- ## What is the Galaxy Technical Board (GTB)? - The GTB represents Galaxy developers and maintainers in the Galaxy governance - Each WG can send a representative to the GTB - The GTB chair is a member of the Galaxy Executive Board --- ## What is the Galaxy Executive Board (GEB)? - The GEB is responsible for the overall direction and coordination of the Galaxy Project ---
